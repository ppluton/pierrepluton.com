# Direction approved in conversation

Personal portfolio for sites, design, applications and AI integrations. Four real projects, a small AI selection, direct Malt contact. No forms, accounts or backend. Domain pierrepluton.com confirmed. Local build only; no publication requested yet.

Visual system: paper #f8f9fc, ink #24204c, electric violet #5740ed, pale lilac #e9e5ff, apricot #ffbe98. Heavy Manrope type for display and text. An oversized hero and a dimensional orbital mark introduce the developer/designer identity. Asymmetric image surfaces present authentic public product visuals. The warm restaurant imagery contrasts with the technical identity.

Layout: compact horizontal header; oversized left-aligned statement with orbital sculpture at right; staggered work grid; service accordion; IA project list; large contact section. Mobile becomes one column and removes pointer-only effects.

Motion: hero arrival, orbital rotation, pointer-following depth, scroll-linked image movement and progress, intentional section reveals, tactile button responses. No scroll hijacking. Persistent motion control and OS reduced-motion support. Content remains visible without JS.

Self-review: avoid generic dark/neon-green agency styling, fake browser screens and claims of business uplift. Hero uses a real project preview, with decorative geometry separate from product evidence. Hotel concept omitted until actually built.
